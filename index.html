💡 Beginner tips
  • Always set a stop loss (S/L). No S/L, no trade.
  • Use 0.01 lot on a small account.
  • Take profit (T/P) about 2× your stop distance.
  • Don’t use a stop tighter than $10. Gold moves too much.
  • After the trade is +$10, move your S/L to break-even (your entry price).
  • Avoid 8:30–10:30 PM PH time and news times.
  • Delete unfilled pending orders before news.
  • Close your trades before the weekend.
  • A limit order only fills if price reaches it. No touch, no trade.
